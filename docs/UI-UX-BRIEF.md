# UI/UX Design System Brief

## Urban Flood Intelligence Platform

### 1. Aesthetic Philosophy
- **Precision Data-Dense Slate**: Engineered for command-center environments and high-stress civil protection workflows.
- **High-Legibility Minimalism**: Contrast ratios strictly compliant with WCAG AAA standards.
- **Brand Identity**: Teal / Emerald (`--brand-primary: #0f766e`, `--brand-secondary: #14b8a6`).
- **Operational Risk Separation**: Risk colors (`--risk-low`, `--risk-moderate`, `--risk-high`, `--risk-critical`) are strictly isolated from the brand palette to prevent cognitive confusion.

### 2. Typography Hierarchy
- **Body & Headings**: Inter (Display: 36px, H1: 30px, H2: 24px, H3: 20px, H4: 18px, Body Large: 16px, Body: 15px, Body Small: 14px, Caption: 12px, Label: 13px).
- **Telemetry & Metrics**: JetBrains Mono (Metric: 24px tabular figures).

### 3. Spacing Rhythm
- 4px baseline grid unit: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, 48, 56, 64px. No arbitrary spacing.
